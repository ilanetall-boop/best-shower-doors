What Could Be Better

The single size option is the biggest limitation. Comfystyle sells the Neo Angle Frameless door in one size, 36 by 72 inches, while the ACE DECOR covers a 32 to 36 inch range and the GETPRO covers 68 to 72 inches. If your corner opening falls outside 36 inches, this door will not work no matter how much you like the price.

The listing also does not publish glass thickness or hardware finish, two details that matter for a frameless door where the glass itself carries the structural load. We would confirm both directly with the seller before ordering, since neither spec appears in the product data we reviewed.
