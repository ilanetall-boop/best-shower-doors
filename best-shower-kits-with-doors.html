Frequently Asked Questions

What's the real difference between a shower door kit and a shower curtain with a door print?

A shower door kit in the strict sense is a hinged or pivoting glass panel, like the three Royal Guard, Comfystyle, and Noirpierre doors compared here, that seals against a wall opening. A shower curtain printed with a barn door design, like the Dazzlewall and NYMB picks, gives you the visual of a door for under $17 but doesn't seal the same way and needs replacing periodically like any fabric curtain.

How do I know what size shower door kit I need?

Measure your rough wall opening at the top, middle, and bottom before shopping. The pivot doors in this guide cover openings from 24 to 25.4 inches up to 56 to 60 inches, and going outside a door's listed adjustment range, usually a few inches per side, means it won't seal properly against the wall.

Can I fix a childproofing problem without replacing my whole shower door?

Yes, if the door itself is intact and the problem is a toddler or pet sliding it open. A no-drill lock sleeve like the Juphex pick in this guide slides over the edge of the glass and costs a fraction of a full door replacement, as long as you order the size that matches your glass thickness.
