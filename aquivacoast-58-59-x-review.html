Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AquivaCoast 58-59" x 72" Frameless easy to install on an out-of-plumb wall?
Yes, within limits. It allows up to 1 inch of adjustability on a flat wall and about 0.39 inch (10mm) of depth adjustment for a wall that is out of plumb, which covers most minor framing irregularities without cutting the glass. A wall that is more than 10mm out of plumb will need shimming or a different solution.
How wide is the walk-in opening on the AquivaCoast compared to the ComfyStyle and BILVOS?
The AquivaCoast's walk-in opening measures 25.1 inches, wider than the ComfyStyle's 24-to-28 inch range at its narrowest setting and considerably wider than the BILVOS's 17.25-to-21.25 inch opening. That gap is the main reason this AquivaCoast 58 59" x review recommends it specifically for 58-to-59 inch stalls.
